In XR environments, users can interact with applications through a variety of input methods. This section outlines guidelines for [Controller](Mapping.md), [Voice](Voice.md), [Gaze](Gaze.md) und [Gesture](Gestures.md)-based interactions, each offering different advantages depending on the context of use. \[[34](References.md#ref-34), [64](References.md#ref-64), [76](References.md#ref-76), [82](References.md#ref-82)\] The choice of input method can be influenced by factors such as the type of application, the nature of the interactable objects, user preferences, and the required level of precision or immersion. \[[76](References.md#ref-76), [82](References.md#ref-82)\] In addition to interaction techniques, controller mapping is also addressed, including considerations for locomotion.
