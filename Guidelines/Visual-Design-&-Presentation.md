---
title: Visual Design & Presentation
---
Visual design plays a key role in usability, immersion, and clarity. This category addresses how information is presented through [Diegetic and Non-Diegetic](https://github.com/theorchestrator/eac-ux-guidelines/blob/main/Guidelines/Visual-Design-&-Presentation/Diegetic-and-Non-Diegetic-Interfaces.md) elements such as menus and in-world object interactions. The section [Colours](https://github.com/theorchestrator/eac-ux-guidelines/blob/main/Guidelines/Visual-Design-&-Presentation/Colours.md) provides insights into colour theory and recommendations for appropriate colour use, while [Space Lighting Conditions](https://github.com/theorchestrator/eac-ux-guidelines/blob/main/Guidelines/Visual-Design-&-Presentation/Space-Lighting-Conditions.md) focuses on design adaptations for extreme lighting conditions, such as deep shadows or intense brightness.
