---
title: User Comfort
---
User comfort is essential for sustained engagement and a positive XR experience. This category focuses on reducing physical, visual, and cognitive strain to ensure users can interact comfortably for longer periods. By addressing factors such as [Visual Fatigue](https://github.com/theorchestrator/eac-ux-guidelines/blob/main/Guidelines/User-Comfort/Visual-Fatigue.md), [Motion Sickness](https://github.com/theorchestrator/eac-ux-guidelines/blob/main/Guidelines/User-Comfort/Motion-Sickness.md), and diverse user needs (i.e. [Accessibility](https://github.com/theorchestrator/eac-ux-guidelines/blob/main/Guidelines/User-Comfort/Accessibility.md)), designers can create experiences that are inclusive, safe, and accessible for a wide range of users.
